Scope: V3 admin panel visual concept. Visitor mode: Operate. The owner glances at it at night beside Discord, OBS and a terminal.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A restrained operating tool of see-through dark glass over a real picture, layered by surface weight, with one line of thought drawn through it. Refuses floating panels, oversized headings, big-number cards, soft-shadow rounded cards and more than two accents.

OWN-WORLD: An owner-chosen picture kept local, default brightness(.66). Content surface defaults to rgba(9,10,16,.105) over blur(7px) saturate(1.5) brightness(.68), adjustable in an appearance popover. Sidebar and right rail flush to the window edges in heavier glass, default rgba(9,9,15,.31) over blur(14px). A darker band holds the thought axis and scene switch. Geist. Two accents only: pink #f29dc4 primary, violet #7355e8 secondary, both solid on controls; neutrals for everything else; red #d6373f only for the emergency stop.

STORY: The owner sees at once what she is doing and whether anything is wrong, then follows one event from what she heard to what she did.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Overview only: a short opening on the picture with one status line, then the thought axis and metrics in the content surface beside a right rail of things to act on. Other pages start directly at the content surface.

FORM: Owner-pinned direction: AIRI official site and Vercel Geist glass over an owner-supplied picture, restrained, two accents. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
